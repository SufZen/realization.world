# Voice and style

Applies to every script, caption and article produced by the content engine.
Source: `docs/strategy/realization-content-automation-strategy.html` §9, §18, plus the published field notes in `src/content/site.ts`.

## Two identities, one person

| | Realization (brand) | Asaf / Suf Zen (founder) |
|---|---|---|
| Speaks as | "we" — the studio | "I" — the operator |
| Says | What we know, build, study, do | How I think, what I learned, what I believe |
| Channels | YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, realization.world/insights | LinkedIn, X, Facebook |
| Tone | Calm, exact, institutional | Direct, reflective, occasionally contrarian |

When an insight fits both, write two native versions. Never cross-post identical copy.

## Spoken, not written

Scripts are read aloud by the avatar. Write the way Asaf talks across a table.

- Avoid: "The feasibility assessment of a real estate development requires the analysis of several interdependent factors."
- Prefer: "Here's something developers learn very quickly. A building can work perfectly on paper, and still be a terrible development."

Rules:

1. One idea per sentence. Most sentences under 15 words.
2. Concrete before abstract: the situation first, then the principle.
3. Numbers only when verified and cleared (see privacy). Otherwise generalize: "a noticeable share of the budget".
4. Name the trade-off. Every strong piece says what you give up.
5. End with a decision rule the viewer can use tomorrow.

## What we never do

- Guru or hype language: "game-changer", "unlock your potential", "10x", "secret", "nobody tells you".
- Fake luxury: skyscraper montages, sports cars, staged rich-office sets.
- Invented experience. If it did not happen in our work, it is not "a case we had".
- Identifiable private cases, counterparties, investors, prices, negotiations or disputes.
- Generic AI-account content: if anyone with a chatbot could post it, it is not ours.

## Hooks that fit us

- The counterintuitive fact: "The cheapest part of a renovation is the part that decides everything."
- The paper-vs-reality gap: "On paper, half of this house doesn't exist."
- The judgment reveal: "Here's the first question I ask about any plot. It's not the price."
- The cost of sequence: "We almost priced a building before it was designed. Here's why we stopped."

## Web articles (realization.world/insights)

Follow the line-break rule in `README.md`: mark breaks in titles and excerpts with ` | `, never end a line on a weak word, one short sentence per line in detail text. Field-note structure: 4–6 short sections, each with a two-to-four-word heading.

## AI disclosure

Avatar content is labelled as AI-generated on every platform that offers the flag. We do not hide the avatar.
